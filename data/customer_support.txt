Password Reset:
Users can reset their password using the Forgot Password option on the login screen.

Refund Policy:
Refund requests are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

Customers can update personal information from the account settings page.

Loan Foreclosure:
Foreclosure charges depend on loan type and remaining tenure.

Customers may apply for a credit card online through the banking portal.

Escalation:
Complex customer complaints may be escalated to human support.
